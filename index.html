About Us

Brewing connections, one Ripple at a time.

Hello folks, we are Jelly & Jo - the sibling duo behind Ripple Grove Coffee! It's been a dream of ours to open our own Speciality Coffee shop and it is now happening! We have both been working in the Edinburgh coffee scene and hospitality for the last twelve years and are excited to put our experience into our own project.

Together, our vision for Ripple Grove is to create an inclusive community hub for creatives, remote workers, friends and family. Somewhere calm & welcoming where you can slow down, catch your breath and connect.

We chose the name Ripple Grove because for us it symbolizes our ethos & values that we want to share which is the small ripples of interaction we create that would hopefully grow into a positive impact in our shared community and beyond. We also both enjoy spending time in nature and how it gives you a boost, so we wanted to create a little grove inside the café & collective space to have a little connection with nature present and to help make it feel calm.

We are LGBTQIA+, Neurodivergent, and Accessible friendly. The café is based in Leith, Edinburgh, sharing the space with 'Collective Spaces' who run a co-working space, events, gallery exhibits, rooms for hire, and a yoga studio.

We look forward to seeing you at Ripple Grove and we hope you become be part of the Ripple!
